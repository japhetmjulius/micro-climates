# Research Questions

## Primary question

How do terrain and environmental characteristics influence localized microclimate patterns within a defined geographic region?

## Secondary questions

### Terrain
1. How strongly does elevation influence temperature?
2. Does slope orientation influence local temperature?
3. How do valleys and ridges influence rainfall?
4. Can terrain features explain persistent localized climate differences?

### Land surface
5. How does vegetation affect local temperature?
6. Does urbanization create measurable local temperature differences?
7. How does land cover interact with rainfall and soil moisture?

### Temporal change
8. Are local temperature patterns changing over time?
9. Are rainfall extremes becoming more or less frequent?
10. Are dry periods changing in duration?
11. Are observed changes consistent across different elevations?

### Prediction
12. Can terrain features improve localized temperature prediction?
13. Can historical conditions improve rainfall anomaly detection?
14. Can machine learning outperform simple statistical baselines?

### Risk
15. Can combinations of rainfall, terrain and soil conditions identify elevated flood susceptibility?
16. Can environmental anomalies be detected before severe impacts occur?
17. Which variables contribute most strongly to each predicted hazard?

### Agriculture
18. Can localized climate information improve seasonal agricultural planning?
19. Can historical climate patterns identify areas vulnerable to drought or excessive rainfall?
20. How much additional predictive value is obtained from terrain and remote-sensing information?

## Research discipline

Each question should eventually have:

```text
Hypothesis
↓
Data
↓
Method
↓
Experiment
↓
Metric
↓
Result
↓
Conclusion
```

A question is not considered answered merely because a graph looks convincing.
